# Please read, there are no in-game instructions!
**Panzercorn** is a first-person arcade shooter where you ride a magic carpet through the valleys of Utopia, wielding rainbow beams against the evil everycorn forces. Blast toadicorns, buttercorns, and more as you work your way up to the dread unicorns!

## 🥽 VR recommended
Panzercorn is made for WebXR-capable VR headsets, with hand tracking or controllers. The game is also playable on desktop or mobile, in landscape.

Once the game loads, tap anywhere to enter VR. Note: there is a bug on Quest where you may need to first hit the JS13k full screen button at the top right, before entering VR!

## 🌈 How to play
Fly your magic carpet vertically and horizontally. Your beam is a lock-on weapon: acquire targets, watch for the red lock glyph above each enemy, then unleash up to five beams at once.

## ✋ VR hands
- Place your **left hand** inside the control orb to your left, palm level, then tilt it side to side like an airplane to steer.
- Hold your hand above the orb to rise; below to descend.
- Your **right hand** controls your weapon: close your fist to begin, look through your hand at enemies to lock, open your fist to fire.

## 🎮 VR controller
- Use the **left stick** to steer the carpet.
- Use the **right trigger** to begin, look through your right hand at enemies to lock, then release the trigger to fire.

## 🖱️ Desktop
- Use **WASD** to fly your carpet.
- Hold the **left mouse button** to begin, hover over enemies to lock, release to fire.

## 📱Mobile
- Tap and drag to move and lock, release to fire!

## 🤫 Spoilers

If you're stuck, here are a few things to know:
* speed and difficulty go up over levels
* Whalicorn is only lockable out of water
* Eaglicorn fires a horizontal beam, you must be above or below
* Unicorn fires directly at you, you must dodge left or right
* barely missing rocks awards multiplier!
* free heart after every boss!
